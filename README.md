# 🧠 Truth Whisperer AI

### Explainable Fake News Detection Platform

> “We don’t just detect fake news — we explain *why* it’s fake.”

---

## 🚀 Overview

**Truth Whisperer AI** is an AI-powered fake news detection platform designed to go beyond simple classification. Instead of just labeling content as *real* or *fake*, it provides **explainable insights, credibility breakdown, and narrative analysis** to help users understand *how misinformation works*.

This project focuses on **transparency, interpretability, and user trust**, making it more than just an AI tool — it’s an educational system.

---

## 🎯 Key Features

### 🧠 Explainable AI Analysis

* Detects whether news is **Real / Fake / Suspicious**
* Highlights **suspicious phrases**
* Provides human-readable explanations:

  * Emotional language
  * Clickbait patterns
  * Lack of credible sources

---

### 🎭 Reality Rewrite Engine 🔥

* Converts sensational or misleading content into a **neutral, factual version**
* Displays **side-by-side comparison**
* Helps users understand how misinformation is framed

---

### 📊 Credibility Radar Chart

Visual breakdown of trust factors:

* Source Trust
* Language Neutrality
* Evidence Strength
* Virality Risk

---

### 🧬 Narrative Pattern Detector

Identifies manipulation techniques:

* Fear-based narratives
* Political bias
* Propaganda tone

---

### 🕒 Truth Evolution Timeline

Shows how misinformation evolves:

* Original claim → Modified version → Viral fake

---

### 🔍 Headline vs Content Mismatch Detector

Detects inconsistency between:

* Headline claims
* Actual article content

---

### 🧾 Trust Breakdown System

Instead of a single score:

* Content Trust
* Source Credibility
* Language Integrity

---

### 📱 Multi-Page Experience

* **Home** → Analyze news
* **Dashboard** → View history & stats
* **Explore** → Demo fake news cases
* **About** → Understand system logic

## 🧪 Demo Workflow

1. Paste news text or URL
2. Click **Analyze**
3. View:

   * Prediction (Fake / Real)
   * Confidence score
   * Highlighted suspicious content
   * Explanation panel
4. Explore advanced insights:

   * Credibility radar
   * Narrative patterns
   * Rewrite to truth

Unlike traditional fake news detectors, this system focuses on:

* **Explainability over black-box AI**
* **User understanding over raw accuracy**
* **Visual storytelling over plain output**
